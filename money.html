The Rules
· Split before you spend. Not after you eat, not after you send someone money. The split happens first. Everything else comes from what remains in life.
· Savings needs a separate bank. If your savings and spending are on the same app, one tap drains it. Different bank, no card attached — that friction is the point.
· Tax is not your money. 15% belongs to the government. You are holding it. Spending it is borrowing from the IRS.
· Ops is for work only. Internet, tools, subscriptions that generate income. Personal phone data for you personally comes from life.
· When life hits zero, you wait. No loan apps. No borrowing from savings. You wait for the next payment. The buffer you're building is so you never have to wait hungry.
Why 15-20-15-50
Freelance income in Nigeria is irregular. Most months you're fine, some months nothing comes in. The standard budgeting advice — built for salaried workers — doesn't apply.

This split is designed for that reality. 50% for life is generous enough to actually live on without grinding. 20% for savings builds the buffer that stops you from needing loan apps. 15% for tax keeps you compliant under the new NTA 2025 rules. 15% for ops keeps the work running without eating into your personal budget.

The goal isn't to live small. It's to never be at zero when a big payment is late.
