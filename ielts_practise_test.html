Writing · Band Descriptors

Compare your essays against these criteria examiners use:

Task Achievement / Response — Did you fully address the task? Task 1: did you cover all key features? Task 2: did you answer all parts of the question?

Coherence & Cohesion — Is your essay logically structured? Do paragraphs flow? Did you use linking words naturally?

Lexical Resource — Did you use a varied and precise vocabulary? Did you avoid repeating the same words?

Grammatical Range & Accuracy — Did you use a mix of sentence structures? Are there many errors?
