Section Tips · What Actually Moves Your Band
👂
Listening · Band 7 target
Read ahead during the 30-second preview. You know the question before you hear the answer — underline keywords. For fill-in-the-blank: spelling counts. Write exactly what you hear. Transfer in the 10 minutes at the end, don't rush during the recording.
📖
Reading · The speed problem
You have 60 min for 3 passages. That's 20 min each. Read the questions FIRST, then skim the passage for answers. TFNG: "Not Given" means the passage doesn't mention it — it is NOT a contradiction. This trips everyone up.
✍️
Writing Task 1 · The most learnable section
Structure: Introduction (paraphrase the question) → Overview (2–3 main trends, no data) → Body 1 (specific data + comparisons) → Body 2 (more detail). Never give your opinion. The overview paragraph alone can push you from Band 5 to Band 7.
📝
Writing Task 2 · Your strongest section
4 paragraphs: Intro (paraphrase + thesis) → Body 1 (first view + example) → Body 2 (second view OR your counterargument) → Conclusion (restate position). 280+ words. Do NOT use bullet points. Vary your sentence length — complex + simple mixed.
🗣️
Speaking · Your best section, probably
You talk well. The examiner is marking fluency (no long pauses), lexical resource (varied words), grammar range, and pronunciation clarity — not your accent. Avoid filler words: "like", "you know", "I mean". Use connectors: "Having said that", "What I mean is", "For instance".
⚡
Quick vocab wins
Task 1: "increased significantly", "remained relatively stable", "peaked at", "there was a sharp decline in", "accounted for X%". Task 2: "It is widely argued that", "proponents of this view contend", "while this perspective has merit", "a more nuanced approach would suggest".

Part 02 · MEXT 2027 Application Roadmap
Your track: Embassy Recommendation (Research Student). This is the standard route for someone with a BSc applying for Masters. You apply through the Japanese Embassy in Abuja, pass a written exam + interview, get recommended to MEXT, then secure a Japanese professor supervisor and university acceptance. The cycle for 2027 intake opens in April 2026 — that is NOW.
The Full Timeline
NOW · March–April 2026
IELTS + Professor Outreach Begins
Write IELTS on April 7. Simultaneously start identifying Japanese professors in your research area whose work aligns with yours. This is the step most people skip and it is the most important one. Email 5–10 professors.
April–May 2026
Embassy Application Window Opens
The Japanese Embassy in Abuja releases the official application forms and guidelines. Check ng.emb-japan.go.jp regularly starting April 1. Download forms the moment they appear. Deadline is typically late May.
May 2026
Submit Embassy Application
Submit: MEXT application form, academic transcripts, graduation certificate, research proposal, 2 reference letters, passport copy, and IELTS score report (or English medium certificate from your university).
June–July 2026
First Screening · Written Exam + Interview
Embassy conducts a written exam (mathematics, science, or your field) plus an interview in English. If you pass, you become a "preliminary candidate" and receive instructions to secure a university acceptance.
August–October 2026
Secure University Acceptance (2 max)
Contact up to 2 Japanese universities for Provisional Acceptance Letters. This is where having already emailed professors pays off enormously. A professor who knows you is far more likely to facilitate an acceptance.
October–December 2026
MEXT Second Screening · Final Selection
Embassy submits recommended candidates to MEXT in Tokyo. MEXT conducts final review. Results announced December 2026 – January 2027.
April or October 2027
You Land in Japan
Spring intake: April 2027. Autumn intake: September/October 2027. MEXT pays your return airfare, tuition, and gives you ~145,000 yen/month stipend.

What Actually Gets You Selected
🔬
Specific Research Plan
Vague plans lose. "I want to study AI" is weak. "I want to investigate federated learning approaches for privacy-preserving recommendation systems, building on the work of Prof. Yamada at Waseda" is strong. Be specific to the point it's uncomfortable.
👨‍🏫
Willing Professor = Massive Advantage
MEXT does not guarantee placement. Candidates who already have a professor interested in them are dramatically more likely to clear the university acceptance stage. Email professors before you even submit your embassy application.
📊
Your Grades Are Good Enough
Second-class upper clears the minimum GPA requirement. You're not disqualified. But if you have any additional credentials (certifications, published work, conference papers, open source projects) include them — they differentiate you.
🇯🇵
"Why Japan" Must Be Real
Examiners can tell generic answers. Japan has genuine strengths in robotics, HCI, manufacturing technology, and certain AI subfields. Know which Japanese researchers are world-class in your area and say why their environment specifically advances your goals.
